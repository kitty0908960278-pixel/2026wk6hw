About

Source Selection

We partner exclusively with certified global orchards that practice sustainable farming and adhere to strict agricultural standards.

Harvest & Sorting

Fruits are harvested at their peak ripeness and meticulously hand-picked. They undergo multi-layered inspections for optimal size, color, and sugar content (Brix level).

Cold Chain Logistics

We maintain an uninterrupted, temperature-controlled cold chain to preserve maximum crispness, flavor, and nutritional value during transit.
